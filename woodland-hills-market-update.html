For sellers: price for the first two weeks

  • The median home sold in 20 days in September. That tells me buyers are still showing up for homes that are priced right.
  • Sellers got a median 98.5% of list price. Homes that start too high usually end up sitting, and then they sell for less.
  • 194 homes are for sale right now. Your home is competing with those listings, not with last spring's sales.
  • Condition and presentation matter more when buyers have choices. I'd rather help you fix the small things before you list than negotiate them later.

Woodland Hills market FAQ

Is Woodland Hills a buyer's or seller's market right now?

As of Oct 31, 2026 there were 194 active single-family listings against an average of 54.33 closed sales per month (Apr–Sep 2026), about 3.57 months of supply. That is roughly a balanced market, leaning slightly toward buyers. Condos were at about 3.95 months.

What is the median home price in Woodland Hills?

In September 2026 the median single-family sale price in Woodland Hills was $1,338,000 ($610.56 per square foot). The median condo/townhouse sale was $650,000.

How do 91364 and 91367 compare?

In September 2026, 91364 had 20 single-family sales at a median of $1,580,000 and 23 median days on market. 91367 had 22 sales at a median of $1,310,000 and 19 median days.

How long does it take to sell a home in Woodland Hills?

The median single-family home that closed in September 2026 sold in 20 days on market. Over the past 12 months the monthly median ranged from 18 to 62 days.

Are home prices dropping in Woodland Hills?

Monthly medians move around depending on which homes sell. September 2026's single-family median ($1,338,000) was below October 2025's ($1,435,000), but across the last 12 months it ranged from $1,197,500 to $1,535,000, with no steady direction. Sellers received a median 98.5% of list price in September.

How is this data calculated?

All figures come from CRMLS Matrix closed sales by month for the City of Woodland Hills, pulled Oct 31, 2026. Months of supply is active listings divided by the average monthly closed sales for April–September 2026. Medians are reported by CRMLS; they are not averages.

Methodology & source

Source: CRMLS Matrix, closed sales by month, City of Woodland Hills; snapshot as of Oct 31, 2026. Deemed reliable but not guaranteed.

Report month: September 2026. Figures cover closed single-family and condo/townhouse sales in the City of Woodland Hills (ZIP codes 91364 and 91367). Months of supply = active listings on Oct 31, 2026 divided by average monthly closed sales April–September 2026 (single-family: 194 / 54.33). Under 3 months is generally a seller's market, 3–6 roughly balanced, over 6 a buyer's market. Year-over-year comparisons are not shown because September 2025 data was not part of this pull.
