For sellers: price for today's buyers

  • The median home sold in 56 days in October — much slower than September's 20-day median. Pricing to the market from day one matters more than ever.
  • Sellers got a median 97.1% of list price. Homes that start too high usually end up sitting, and then they sell for less.
  • 197 homes are for sale right now. Your home is competing with those listings, not with last spring's sales.
  • Condition and presentation matter more when buyers have choices. I'd rather help you fix the small things before you list than negotiate them later.

For buyers: you have options, but good homes still move

  • With about 3.75 months of supply, you have more to choose from than in a tight seller's market, and room to negotiate on homes that have been sitting.
  • October's median was 56 days on market, so you usually have time to compare — but still be pre-approved so you can move when the right home shows up.
  • Ask about seller credits for repairs or a rate buydown. That can help your payment more than a small price cut.

Woodland Hills market FAQ

Is Woodland Hills a buyer's or seller's market right now?

As of Nov 5, 2026 there were 197 active single-family listings against an average of 52.5 closed sales per month (May–Oct 2026), about 3.75 months of supply. That is roughly a balanced market, leaning toward buyers. Condos were at about 4.05 months.

What is the median home price in Woodland Hills?

In October 2026 the median single-family sale price in Woodland Hills was $1,341,000 ($596.05 per square foot). The median condo/townhouse sale was $520,000.

How do 91364 and 91367 compare?

In October 2026, 91364 had 18 single-family sales at a median of $1,403,500 and 71 median days on market. 91367 had 11 sales at a median of $1,325,000 and 23 median days.

How long does it take to sell a home in Woodland Hills?

The median single-family home that closed in October 2026 sold in 56 days on market. Over the past 12 months the monthly median ranged from 18 to 62 days.

Are home prices dropping in Woodland Hills?

Monthly medians move around depending on which homes sell. October 2026's single-family median ($1,341,000) was below October 2025's ($1,435,000), and sales were slower (30 closings vs 54 a year earlier). Across the last 12 months the median ranged from $1,197,500 to $1,535,000, with no steady direction. Sellers received a median 97.1% of list price in October.

How is this data calculated?

All figures come from CRMLS Matrix closed sales by month for the City of Woodland Hills, pulled Nov 5, 2026. Months of supply is active listings divided by the average monthly closed sales for May–October 2026. Medians are reported by CRMLS; they are not averages.

Methodology & source

Source: CRMLS Matrix, closed sales by month, City of Woodland Hills; snapshot as of Nov 5, 2026. Deemed reliable but not guaranteed.

Report month: October 2026. Figures cover closed single-family and condo/townhouse sales in the City of Woodland Hills (ZIP codes 91364 and 91367). Months of supply = active listings on Nov 5, 2026 divided by average monthly closed sales May–October 2026 (single-family: 197 / 52.5 ≈ 3.75; condo/townhouse: 50 / 12.33 ≈ 4.05). Under 3 months is generally a seller's market, 3–6 roughly balanced, over 6 a buyer's market. Year-over-year figures compare October 2026 to October 2025 from the same CRMLS pull.
