How do I find out what my Woodland Hills home is worth?

Online estimates like a Zestimate are a starting point, not a price. The most dependable valuation compares your home with recent nearby sales of similar size, condition and location. Request a private valuation and I’ll prepare one from local comparables.

How long do homes take to sell in Woodland Hills?

Single-family homes in Woodland Hills that closed in October 2026 spent a median of 56 days on the market, compared with 30 days in October 2025. In 91367 alone, the 11 single-family homes that closed in October had a median of 23 days, and condos and townhomes across Woodland Hills took a median of 33 days. Your own timing depends on price, condition and location.

What are homes selling for in Woodland Hills right now?

In October 2026 the median closed sale price for single-family homes in Woodland Hills was $1,341,000, about 6.5% lower than in October 2025 ($1,435,000), and sellers received a median 97.1% of list price. Condos and townhomes closed at a median of $520,000 and 99.5% of list price. Monthly medians move with whichever homes happen to sell, so your home’s value is best judged against similar nearby sales.

Is now a good time to sell my Woodland Hills home?

It depends on your goals and your home. On November 5, 2026 there were 197 single-family homes for sale in Woodland Hills, about 3.75 months of supply at the average monthly sales pace for May to October 2026: roughly a balanced market, leaning toward buyers. Pricing correctly from day one matters most. I’m happy to walk through the current numbers for your street.
