Is My Inherited Woodland Hills Home Still Insured While It Sits Empty?

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated September 2026

If you inherited a Woodland Hills home, there is a risk that gets missed in the middle of everything else: the homeowners insurance may not cover the property the way you expect while it sits empty.

The 30-Day Vacancy Problem

According to CostULess Direct's California vacant home insurance guide (published June 15, 2026), coverage restrictions typically begin after about 30 consecutive days without regular occupancy, and some policies allow up to 60 days in certain situations. Perils often excluded or limited after that point include vandalism and malicious mischief, theft, glass breakage, and water damage from plumbing issues, with coverage sometimes narrowed to named perils such as fire and lightning. Policies also treat "vacant" (no occupants and no furnishings) differently from "unoccupied" (furnished but no daily residents), so the exact wording of the policy matters.

Coverage Does Not Automatically Pass to Heirs

Policygenius (updated May 28, 2024) explains that a homeowners policy typically stays active for roughly 30 days after the policyholder dies, that the insurer usually needs to be notified within that window, and that coverage does not automatically transfer to the heirs. The executor or heirs generally have to contact the insurer and arrange coverage in their own names or for the estate. Policies differ, so this is the general pattern, not a guarantee for any specific policy.

Why the Timeline Matters in Woodland Hills

Movoto's data (updated September 30, 2026) shows Woodland Hills homes averaging 48 days on market compared with 45 a year earlier, with 421 active listings and 71 price-reduced. That is the marketing period alone, before any time spent on probate, clearing the house, repairs, or preparing it to list. A home can easily pass a 30-day vacancy threshold before it is under contract, which is why insurance belongs on the timeline from the start. For the probate side, see whether the small estate affidavit lets you skip probate.

What a Vacant Dwelling Policy Costs

CostULess Direct reports that vacant home insurance typically runs 25% to 50% more than standard homeowners coverage, with estimates from roughly $1,500 to over $5,000 a year depending on location and property value. Those are broad estimates, not quotes for a Woodland Hills address.

What to Do First

Call the insurer early and ask in writing what is covered while the home is empty and what changes after 30 days. Ask whether a vacant dwelling or similar endorsement is available and what it would cost. Keep the property secured and maintained, and keep records of those steps. Then build the sale timeline around those dates. Frequently Asked Questions

Is my inherited house still insured if it is empty?

It depends on the policy. Insurers commonly limit coverage after about 30 consecutive days without regular occupancy, and perils like vandalism, theft, and water damage are often restricted or excluded. Ask the insurer in writing.

Does the homeowners policy automatically pass to the heirs?

Generally no. Coverage typically continues for about 30 days after the owner's death and the insurer usually needs to be notified. The executor or heirs normally have to arrange coverage going forward.

What is a vacant dwelling policy?

A specialized policy for homes left empty longer than a standard policy allows. CostULess Direct reports these typically cost 25% to 50% more than standard coverage, roughly $1,500 to over $5,000 a year.

How long does it take to sell a home in Woodland Hills?

Movoto showed an average of 48 days on market as of September 30, 2026, versus 45 a year earlier. That is before any probate or preparation time.

What is the difference between vacant and unoccupied?

Vacant generally means no occupants and no furnishings. Unoccupied means furnished but without daily residents. Policies can treat them differently, so check the wording.
