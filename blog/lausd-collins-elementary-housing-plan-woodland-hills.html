What the LAUSD Collins Elementary Housing Plan Means for Woodland Hills Homeowners

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated October 2026

Woodland Hills homeowners near the former Collins Elementary campus may soon hear about a large apartment proposal. Here is what is confirmed, what is not, and how to think about it as an owner or a buyer.

What the school board actually did

According to Urbanize LA (October 8, 2026), the Los Angeles Unified School District board met on October 6, 2026 and voted to enter exclusive negotiating agreements with developers for two district-owned sites. In Woodland Hills, the site is the former Collins Elementary School campus at 5717 N. Rudnick Avenue, 6.5 acres, and the selected developer is Affirmed Housing. The proposal is 391 one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments with 550 parking spaces. The second site, a 1.5-acre property at 1049 N. Fairfax Avenue in West Hollywood, is not part of this post.

Who the housing is for

Urbanize LA reports both projects are reserved for renters earning between 30 and 120 percent of the area median income. The article does not say whether LAUSD employees get priority, so I am not assuming they do. Some headlines describe the plan as teacher housing, but the primary source does not confirm that detail.

What is not decided yet

A negotiating agreement is not a final approval. Per Urbanize LA, the teams must still negotiate final development agreements, complete the entitlement process and secure financing, and terms may change. The reported schedule for the Woodland Hills site is a groundbreaking in summer 2029 and completion in summer 2031. Building height was not stated in the article.

What it means for home values

I did not find any study or data measuring how this specific project would affect nearby Woodland Hills home prices, so I am not going to guess. What can be said is factual: it is years from construction, the details can still change, and the current market is moving on other factors. Movoto's Woodland Hills page (updated October 9, 2026) shows a median of $1,299,000 for September sales, 405 active listings, 79 price reductions, and an average of 50 days on market versus 44 a year ago.

What to do if you live or are buying nearby

Sellers should be ready to answer buyer questions with the confirmed facts and the timeline rather than rumor. Buyers should ask about the status of the project at the time they write an offer, since the answer may be different in a year.
