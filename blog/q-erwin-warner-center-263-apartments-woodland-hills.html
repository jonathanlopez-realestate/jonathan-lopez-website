Fencing Goes Up at the 263-Apartment Q Erwin Site in Warner Center: What It Means for Woodland Hills Homeowners

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated October 2026

If you drive past Variel Avenue and Erwin Street in Warner Center, you may notice new fencing. According to Urbanize LA (October 13, 2026), it surrounds the site at 6160 Variel Ave. and 21010 Erwin St., which the report describes as a sign that work is about to begin after "a couple years of quiet in Warner Center."

What is planned

Balaciano Group, the developer behind several Q-branded Warner Center projects (Q Variel, Q Topanga and Q De Soto, per Urbanize), has approvals for a seven-story building with 263 apartments, including 31 live/work units. The apartments sit above a large parking structure. The plans also include more than 51,000 square feet of non-residential space, which could cover the live/work units, up to 10 hotel rooms, 10 commercial spaces, about 13,500 square feet of office and about 6,500 square feet of lobby and leasing office. Newman Garrison + Partners did the entitlement design, and permits now point to TCA Architects.

What is not known

Urbanize does not report a construction start date, a completion date or any affordable units. Fencing is a sign of activity, not a schedule. Plans can also change after approval.

Where it sits

The site is a short walk east of the former Promenade mall, which was recently cleared for a roughly $10 billion mixed-use village anchored by the Rams headquarters (Urbanize). It is in Warner Center, not in the quieter single-family streets of west Woodland Hills.

What it means for a homeowner

I could not find data measuring how a single apartment building affects nearby single-family home values, so I am not going to claim it helps or hurts. What I can say is that more rental supply in Warner Center gives some would-be buyers another option, and that this is one of several projects moving in the area. The practical move is to look at what homes near you are actually selling for. Movoto (updated October 13, 2026) shows a $1,299,000 median sale price for September, 380 active listings, 73 of them price-reduced, 50 average days on market versus 44 a year ago, and 389 sales in September versus 327 a year earlier.

What to do with this

If you are thinking of selling, your pricing should rest on recent sold comparables near your address, not on headlines. If you are thinking of buying, ask what is planned within a few blocks of any home you like, and check the city's project pages for the specific parcels nearby.
