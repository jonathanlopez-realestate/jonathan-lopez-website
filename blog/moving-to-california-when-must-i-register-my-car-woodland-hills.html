Moving to California: When Must I Register My Car, and Does Buying or Leasing a Woodland Hills Home Start the Clock?

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated October 2026

Woodland Hills draws a steady flow of people relocating from other states, and the vehicle deadline is one of the first surprises. Per the California DMV (page last modified May 28, 2026), when California registration is required, the owner must apply for original registration within 20 days of the date it became due, or face penalties (CVC 4152.5). The DMV's main residency test is being in California on more than a temporary or transient basis for 6 months or more in a 12-month period (CVC 516). It also lists other indicators: registering to vote here, working here, having a place of business here, paying resident tuition or enrolling dependents in California schools, claiming a homeowner's exemption or leasing a residence, and holding a California driver license. For a vehicle already registered out of state, fees become due on the earliest of several dates, including when residency is established and when the out-of-state registration expires. The DMV page lists no fee amounts and refers to its online fee calculator. This is general information, not legal or DMV advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to register my car after moving to California?

Per the DMV, within 20 days of the date registration became due, or penalties may apply.

What makes me a California resident?

The DMV's main test is 6 months or more in a 12-month period on more than a temporary basis, with other indicators listed.

Does buying or leasing a home matter?

The DMV lists claiming a homeowner's exemption or leasing a residence as indicators. I found no DMV statement that closing alone triggers registration.

How much will it cost?

The DMV page gives no amounts; use its online fee calculator.

Do military members have different rules?

The page says active-duty members and nonresident spouses may be exempt from the vehicle license fee portion via form REG 5045.
