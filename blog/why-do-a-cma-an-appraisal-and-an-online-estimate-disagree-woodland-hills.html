Why Do a CMA, an Appraisal, and an Online Estimate Give Me Different Values for My Woodland Hills Home?

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated October 2026

Owners in Woodland Hills often collect three different numbers for the same house: an online estimate, an agent's opinion of value, and eventually a lender's appraisal. When they disagree, it feels like someone is wrong. Usually each number is answering a different question.

CMA vs. appraisal

According to Zillow, a comparative market analysis is completed by an agent, and an appraisal is performed by an unbiased licensed appraiser. An appraisal is usually required by the lender during escrow to get the loan finalized. In plain terms, a CMA helps you choose a list price, and an appraisal tells a lender whether the property supports the loan amount.

What goes into a CMA

Zillow notes the most accurate comparable homes are typically sold within the past three to six months. Good comps share the same neighborhood, similar bedroom and bathroom counts, similar usable square footage, similar age unless one was substantially updated, and similar finishes and upkeep. Sold prices carry more weight than asking prices.

Why the comp window matters in 91367 right now

Per Movoto (updated September 30, 2026), 91367 shows 222 active listings, 41 price-reduced, and an average of 41 days on market, up from 33 a year ago, with 212 sales in August versus 177 a year earlier. A sale that closed several months ago may reflect a faster market than the one your home would meet today. That is not a prediction, only a reason to weigh the newest closings and current competing listings alongside older sales.

What to bring to the conversation

Any updates to your home, lot, or pool, and any permitted additions. Small differences from comps can move the number, and a good CMA explains each adjustment.
