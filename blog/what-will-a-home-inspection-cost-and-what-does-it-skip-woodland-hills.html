What Will a Home Inspection Cost Me in Woodland Hills, and What Does It Skip?

Jonathan Lopez, Jim Sandoval Group | Woodland Hills Realtor | CA DRE #02282844 · Updated October 2026

A home inspection is a visual review of a property's major systems by a licensed inspector, and it is one of the first costs first-time buyers in Woodland Hills want to size up.

What it costs

Rocket Mortgage (updated August 6, 2026), citing HomeAdvisor data from October 2025, says inspections usually run around $343 nationally, with most homeowners paying $296 to $424, and lists Los Angeles at $330. That is a Los Angeles figure, not a Woodland Hills one. Size matters: $200 to $250 for homes under 1,000 square feet and $300 to $500 for 2,500 to 3,000. Older properties generally cost more to inspect.

What the standard report may not include

The article lists a sewer scope at $270 to $1,734, radon testing at $250 and a termite inspection at $75 to $325 as add-ons. These are national ranges and local quotes will differ. Adding the Los Angeles average to those ranges gives roughly $675 to $2,389 for a standard inspection plus a sewer scope and termite check, my own arithmetic.

Is it required?

Rocket Mortgage says inspections are generally not required by law, though a loan program or purchase contract can require one. That does not make them optional in practice for most buyers.

Local context

Movoto (updated October 22, 2026) shows a Woodland Hills median sale price of $1,299,000 for September, 50 days on market versus 44 a year earlier, and 72 of 377 listings price-reduced.
